Stretch bending — physical process
The pipe is clamped at both ends.
Both ends are held by gripping jaws.
The pipe cannot slip during forming.
Axial tension is applied.
The machine pulls the pipe longitudinally.
This introduces a controlled tensile strain.
The tension reduces compressive stresses during bending.
The pipe is wrapped around a bending form (die).
While under tension, the bending head rotates.
Only the active bending zone changes curvature.
Straight regions before and after the die remain straight.
The pipe reaches the fully loaded shape.
Maximum bending curvature is present.
Axial tension is still applied.
This corresponds to your LoadedHold stage.
The bending load is released.
The pipe elastically recovers.
Curvature decreases due to springback.
Axial tension is gradually removed.
The finished part is unclamped.
The remaining shape is the final geometry.

Bend while stretched	Forming
